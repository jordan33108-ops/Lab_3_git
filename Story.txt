The neon glow of Sector 7’s slums always felt a world away from the starry skies of Nibelheim, but for Tifa Lockhart, it was the place she had to call home. Serving drinks at Seventh Heaven, she wiped down the wooden counter, her mind drifting between the quiet girl she used to be and the fierce fighter she had become. The leather gloves on her hands weren’t just for show; they were a testament to her survival, forged from the ashes of her hometown. When Cloud Strife walked through her bar doors, a ghost from her past wrapped in the uniform of her greatest enemy, Tifa’s world shifted. She saw the heavy burden he carried, a fractured mind hidden behind a cold, mercenary exterior. Joining AVALANCHE wasn’t just about fighting Shinra’s life-draining Mako reactors anymore; it became a deeply personal mission to keep Cloud anchored to reality and to protect the planet from the catastrophic threat of Sephiroth. Her journey took her far beyond the rusted plates of Midgar. From the sunny shores of Costa del Sol to the snowy peaks of the northern continent, Tifa fought with unmatched grace and devastating martial arts. Yet, her greatest battle wasn’t fought with her fists. It took place deep within the Lifestream, where she dove into Cloud’s fractured psyche. With unwavering empathy and shared memories of their childhood water tower, she helped him piece together his true identity. Tifa’s path was defined by resilience. She stood as the emotional heart of her companions, balancing fierce combat prowess with a deeply protective nature. As the planet finally healed, she stood alongside her friends, ready to rebuild a brighter, peaceful future from the ruins of the old world.
