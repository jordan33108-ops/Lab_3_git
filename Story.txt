The neon glow of Sector 7’s slums always felt a world away from the starry skies of Nibelheim, but for Tifa Lockhart, it was the place she had to call home. Serving drinks at Seventh Heaven, she wiped down the wooden counter, her mind drifting between the quiet girl she used to be and the fierce fighter she had become. The leather gloves on her hands weren’t just for show; they were a testament to her survival, forged from the ashes of her hometown. When Cloud Strife walked through her bar doors, a ghost from her past wrapped in the uniform of her greatest enemy, Tifa’s world shifted. She saw the heavy burden he carried, a fractured mind hidden behind a cold, mercenary exterior. Joining AVALANCHE wasn’t just about fighting Shinra’s life-draining Mako reactors anymore; it became a deeply personal mission to keep Cloud anchored to reality and to protect the planet from the catastrophic threat of Sephiroth. With a swift, brutal kick, Cloud shattered Tifa's guard, sending her plunging into the dark abyss of the lower sectors. He hadn't lost his memory; he had been completely consumed by Sephiroth's will from the moment he arrived in Midgar, acting as the ultimate Trojan horse to wipe out the resistance from the inside.Miraculously surviving the fall into the church flowers below, Tifa awoke bruised but unbroken. The betrayal stung deeper than any physical wound, but it ignited a fierce new resolve within her. She didn't just have to save the planet anymore; she had to hunt down the man she once trusted, break Sephiroth's psychic grip on his mind, and drag Cloud back to reality—even if she had to beat it into him.
